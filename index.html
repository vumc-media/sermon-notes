The Grave Couldn’t Hold Him

Luke 24:1-12

1. Resurrection Begins Where Hope Ends

The women came to the tomb expecting death, not resurrection. They brought spices, not celebration. What they expected to be the end became the place where God revealed a new beginning. The resurrection reminds us that God is still working even when all signs seem to point to loss, silence, and disappointment.

Reflection Challenge: Where have you been tempted to give up? What situation feels sealed, finished, or hopeless? Invite Jesus into that place and ask Him to help you trust that He is still at work.

2. The Resurrection Changes Everything

The empty tomb is not just good news for one morning long ago. It changes how we face fear, grief, sin, doubt, and death right now. Because Jesus is alive, despair does not get the last word. The resurrection gives us a living hope, a new identity, and a reason to keep walking by faith.

Reflection Challenge: If you truly lived this week as though Jesus is alive and present with you, what would change in your attitude, your priorities, your courage, or your obedience?
