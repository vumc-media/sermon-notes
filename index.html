When It All Falls Apart: The God Who Speaks

Date: January 18

Scripture: Job 38:1

“And for thirty-seven chapters, God is silent.”
“The Lord answered Job out of the whirlwind.” (Job 38:1)

God Speaks — Without Explaining

When God finally speaks, He does not explain suffering.

He does not answer Job’s questions.

Instead, He asks His own.

“Who is this darkening counsel with words lacking knowledge? Prepare yourself like a man; I will interrogate you, and you will respond to me. Where were you when I laid the earth’s foundations?”

Reflection:

When life falls apart, what answers are you demanding from God?

Creation Still Speaks When God Is Silent

“The heavens are telling the glory of God.” — Psalm 19

Even when God feels silent, creation continues to testify.

Reflection:

Where have you seen God’s fingerprints even when His voice felt quiet?

God’s Perspective Is Bigger Than Our Pain

“My thoughts are not your thoughts.” — Isaiah

God’s silence is not absence. God’s mystery is not neglect.

Reflection:

Where might God be working beyond what you can see or understand?

Jesus Speaks the Same Way

Jesus calms storms without explanation.

“Who then is this?”

Reflection:

Has your faith been shaped more by explanations—or by encounters?

Awe Reorients Us When Life Falls Apart

When life collapses, worship lifts our eyes above the noise.

Reflection:

What would it look like to choose awe over answers this week?

God Reminds Job He Is Not Alone in the Story

The whirlwind is not chaos — it is where God speaks.

Final Reflection:

Where do you need to trust that God is still present, even without answers?
