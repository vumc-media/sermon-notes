When Standing Costs Everything

Acts 7

September 27, 2026

Standing Firm

Acts 7 introduces us to Stephen at one of the most difficult moments imaginable. His faithfulness to Jesus places him in direct conflict with those around him.

Stephen's story invites us to consider what it means to remain faithful when obedience is difficult, costly, or unpopular.

Point 1

Standing firm means obeying God even when it costs you.

Acts 7:51–54

Look again at Acts 7:51–54. Stephen refuses to abandon the truth simply because speaking it has become costly. His obedience is not determined by how people respond to him.

Reflect

Where is following Jesus requiring courage from me right now?
Am I ever tempted to choose what is comfortable over what I know God is asking me to do?

My Notes

Point 2

Standing firm means keeping your eyes on Jesus.

Acts 7:55–56

Look again at Acts 7:55–56. In the middle of hostility and uncertainty, Stephen looks toward heaven. His circumstances have not changed—but his attention remains fixed on Jesus.

Reflect

What tends to capture my attention when life becomes difficult?
What would it look like for me to intentionally keep my eyes on Jesus this week?

My Notes

This Week

Stephen's faithfulness challenges us to think beyond a faith that is convenient. Following Jesus sometimes requires us to stand when standing has a cost.

What is one step of faithful obedience God may be calling me to take this week?
