1. Envy Pollutes the Heart

James 3:14–16

“If you have bitter envy and selfish ambition in your heart… there you find disorder and every kind of evil.”

Envy is not harmless. Scripture exposes it as spiritual rot—earthly, unspiritual, and destructive.

Reflection:
Where might envy be hiding in my heart—disguised as ambition, frustration, or comparison?

2. Envy Poisons Your Peace

Proverbs 14:30

“A peaceful heart gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones.”

Envy steals joy, contentment, and rest. Peace and envy cannot coexist.

Reflection:
How has comparison affected my peace lately?

3. Envy Distorts Your Vision

Mark 15:9–11

Pilate knew the truth: Jesus was handed over out of envy.

Envy blinded religious leaders to the presence of God right in front of them.

Reflection:
Is envy shaping how I see others—or how I see Jesus?

Closing Application

  • Rejoice with those who rejoice — clap loudest for others
  • Recognize what you have — gratitude opens your eyes
  • Give thanks daily — envy fades where thankfulness grows
