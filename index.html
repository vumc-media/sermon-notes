Acts 28:23–31 | Common English Bible

The final chapter of Acts is not the end of God's mission. The story continues through ordinary people who answer God's call.

Point 1 — God's Mission Doesn't Stop When Our Plans Do

Scripture: Acts 28:23–28

Paul arrived in Rome under circumstances he had not planned. Yet he continued sharing the message of Jesus and the kingdom of God.

Some people believed, while others refused to listen. Even disagreement and opposition could not bring God's mission to an end.

Reflect: When have unexpected circumstances changed your plans? Could God still be working through that situation?
Point 2 — The Story Continues When We Say Yes

Scripture: Acts 28:30–31

Acts closes with Paul welcoming people, preaching God's kingdom, and teaching about Jesus Christ with confidence.

The final word of the book emphasizes that the message continued unhindered. The account ends, but the Church's mission does not.

Reflect: What might happen if we stopped waiting for the perfect opportunity and simply said yes to God's call?
