ACTS: The Church That Changed the World

Nobody Is Too Far Gone

Acts 9:1–31
Saul begins Acts 9 as an enemy of the church. By the end of the chapter, he is proclaiming the very Jesus he once opposed. His story reminds us that grace can reach people in places we may have already given up on.
1

Grace Meets Us Before We Get Our Act Together

Saul wasn't searching for Jesus on the road to Damascus. He was actively working against Him. Yet Jesus met Saul right in the middle of who he was and what he was doing.

Grace did not wait for Saul to become worthy of an encounter with Christ. The encounter came first. Transformation followed.

Read: Acts 9:1–6
Notice where Saul was headed and what he intended to do when Jesus interrupted his journey.

Reflect

2

Transformed People Need People Who Believe Grace Is Real

Saul's encounter with Jesus changed him, but the church understandably struggled to believe that change was real. They remembered who Saul had been.

Then Barnabas stepped forward. He saw what God's grace was doing in Saul and was willing to stand beside him.

Read: Acts 9:26–28
Pay attention to the disciples' reaction to Saul and what Barnabas does next.
