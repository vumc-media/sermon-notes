Faith in the Fog

Trusting God When You Can't See the Road Ahead

Still Loved Sermon Series

Psalm 23 | John 11:1-44

Scripture Focus

Psalm 23 reminds us that the Shepherd remains with us in life’s darkest valleys.

John 11:1-44 reminds us that even when Jesus seems delayed, He is still at work.

Big Idea

Faith is not having all the answers. Faith is trusting God when the road ahead is hidden by fog. Even when God feels silent, delayed, or distant, He is still present, still working, and still leading.

Point 1: God’s Delays Are Not God’s Denials

Scripture: John 11:1-44

Mary and Martha thought Jesus was late. They believed that if Jesus had arrived sooner, Lazarus would not have died. But what looked like abandonment was actually preparation for a greater miracle.
Fill in the Blank:

God’s __________ are not God’s __________.

Reflection Questions

  • Have you ever felt like God was taking too long?
  • What prayers are you still waiting for God to answer?
  • How might God be working beyond what you can currently see?

Point 2: The Shepherd Walks With You Through the Valley

Scripture: Psalm 23

Psalm 23 does not promise that we avoid every valley. It promises that the Shepherd walks with us through the valley. The comfort is not that life is always easy. The comfort is that we are never alone.
Fill in the Blank:

The Shepherd walks __________ me through the valley.

Reflection Questions

  • What valley are you currently walking through?
  • Where have you seen God’s presence recently?
  • What would trusting the Shepherd look like this week?

Personal Application

When you cannot see the whole road, follow the Shepherd one step at a time.

Prayer Requests

Closing Prayer

Lord, when life feels uncertain and the road ahead is hidden, help me trust You. Remind me that Your delays are not denials and that Your presence never leaves me. Give me courage in the valley, peace in the waiting, and faith to follow You one step at a time. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
