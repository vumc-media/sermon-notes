Why Two

Luke 15:1–7

The Parable of the Lost Sheep

Opening Thought

Jesus tells the story of a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine sheep to search for one that is lost. His message is clear: lost people matter to God.

The mission is simple: Love God. Love People.

1. Loved People Love People

1 John 4:19 — “We love because he first loved us.”

Before we can genuinely love others, we must first understand that we are deeply loved by God. God’s love comes first. When we receive that love, it changes how we see people.

Reflection

2. Saved People Serve People

Mark 10:45 — “For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve...”

Jesus never viewed people as interruptions. He stopped for the hurting, welcomed the overlooked, and served with humility. A saved heart becomes a serving heart.

Reflection

3. Found People Find People

John 1:40–42

After Andrew met Jesus, he went and found his brother Simon and brought him to Jesus. He did not have all the answers. He simply invited someone to meet Christ.

Reflection

Digging Deeper

Luke 15:1–7

Closing Challenge

Loved people love people.

Saved people serve people.

Found people find people.

This week, ask yourself:

Who can I love? Who can I serve? Who can I help find Jesus?

Prayer

Lord, thank You for loving me when I was lost. Help me to love others as You have loved me. Give me a servant’s heart, and help me point people toward You. Amen.
