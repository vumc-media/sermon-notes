The Grave Couldn’t Hold Him

Luke 24:1-12

1. Resurrection Begins Where Hope Ends

The women came to the tomb expecting death, not resurrection. They brought spices, not celebration. God often does His greatest work in the places where we feel like hope has died.

Where in your life have you given up hope? What if God is not finished yet?

2. The Resurrection Changes Everything

The empty tomb is not just a moment in history—it changes how we live today. Fear becomes faith. Doubt becomes belief. Death gives way to life.

What would change in your life if you truly lived like Jesus is alive?
