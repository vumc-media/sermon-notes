INTO THE WILDERNESS

The Garden Decision

March 15, 2026
Luke 22:39–46
Versailles United Methodist Church

Main Scripture

39 Jesus left and made his way to the Mount of Olives, as was his custom, and the disciples followed him. 40 When he arrived, he said to them, “Pray that you won’t give in to temptation.” 41 He withdrew from them about a stone’s throw, knelt down, and prayed. 42 He said, “Father, if it’s your will, take this cup of suffering away from me. However, not my will but your will must be done.” 43 Then a heavenly angel appeared to him and strengthened him. 44 He was in anguish and prayed even more earnestly. His sweat became like drops of blood falling on the ground. 45 When he got up from praying, he went to the disciples. He found them asleep, overcome by grief. 46 He said to them, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so that you won’t give in to temptation.”

1. Jesus Was Honest About His Pain

Luke 22:42a

“Father, if it’s your will, take this cup of suffering away from me...”

Before Jesus surrendered to the cross, He acknowledged the weight of what He was facing. In the garden, Jesus did not pretend everything was fine. He brought His pain honestly before the Father.

Key Truth: Faith does not mean denying pain. Faith means bringing our pain to God.
  • Jesus was honest, not fake.
  • Jesus was prayerful, not distant.
  • Jesus shows us that honesty before God is not weakness.

2. Jesus Chose Surrender Over Control

Luke 22:42b–43

“However, not my will but your will must be done.”

This is the turning point in the garden. Jesus did not cling to control. He surrendered Himself completely to the Father’s will.

Key Truth: Surrender is not weakness. Surrender is trust.
  • Jesus understood the cost.
  • Jesus still chose obedience.
  • God did not remove the suffering, but God gave strength to endure it.

Verse 43 reminds us: Heaven met Jesus in His surrender.

The Battle Was Won in Prayer

Before the arrest, before the trial, and before the cross, Jesus fought the real battle in prayer. The disciples, however, slept through the moment.

Key Truth: Prayer prepares us for battles we cannot yet see.
  • Jesus prayed with urgency.
  • The disciples slept in grief and weakness.
  • Prayer is one of the ways God strengthens us when temptation and pressure come.

Reflection Questions

1. What situation in your life feels like a “garden moment” right now?
2. Are you trying to control the outcome, or are you surrendering it to God?
3. What would it look like for you to be fully honest with God in prayer this week?

Personal Response

Closing Prayer

Lord, in moments when life feels overwhelming, remind us of the garden. Give us the courage to be honest with You about our pain. Teach us to trust Your will even when we do not understand it. Strengthen us through prayer so that we do not fall into temptation. Help us to surrender our lives to You just as Jesus surrendered in the garden. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
