1

The Holy Spirit Is God’s Presence With Us

Pentecost reminds us that God did not leave His people to follow Jesus on their own. Through the Holy Spirit, God is present with His people — dwelling with us, working within us, and reminding us that we never walk the life of faith alone.

Message Notes

Reflect

  1. Where do I most need to become aware of God’s presence in my life right now?
  2. Are there places in my everyday life where I tend to live as though God is distant or absent?
  3. How would my attitude, decisions, or relationships change if I lived each day fully aware that the Holy Spirit is present with me?

My Reflection

2

The Holy Spirit Empowers Us for Other People

The Spirit’s power was never intended to stop with the people who received it. At Pentecost, ordinary followers of Jesus were empowered to communicate the good news beyond themselves. The Holy Spirit equips us not simply for our own spiritual experience, but so that the love and grace of Jesus can reach other people through us.

Message Notes

Reflect

  1. Who has God placed in my life that I may be called to encourage, serve, love, or reach?
  2. Am I asking the Holy Spirit only to help me, or am I allowing the Spirit to use me for the sake of other people?
  3. What fear, hesitation, or comfort zone might I need the Holy Spirit to help me move beyond?

My Reflection

My Next Step This Week

The Holy Spirit is present with us and empowers us to live beyond ourselves. Identify one specific way you can respond to what God is showing you this week.

Prayer Response

Holy Spirit, make me aware of Your presence. Fill the places in me that I have tried to manage on my own. Give me courage, wisdom, and power to live beyond myself. Show me the people You have placed around me, and use my words, actions, gifts, and life to make the love of Jesus known. Amen.
