Open Hands

Week 7
Acts 4:32–5:11

Today’s Scripture

In Acts 4, we see a church marked by extraordinary generosity, unity, and open-handed living. But as the story moves into Acts 5, we also encounter Ananias and Sapphira—and a sobering reminder that God is concerned not only with what we give, but with the condition of the heart behind it.

Acts 4:32–5:11

As you listen today, consider what this passage reveals about generosity, honesty, community, and the condition of our hearts before God.

Read Acts 4:32–37 →
Read Acts 5:1–11 →
Point 1

Open Hands Begin With an Open Heart

The generosity of the early church was more than a financial practice. It flowed from a community whose hearts had been transformed by Jesus.

Their willingness to share what they had demonstrated that they understood their possessions differently. What God had placed in their hands could become a blessing in the hands of someone else.

Reflect What might I be holding too tightly that God is inviting me to hold with open hands?
Point 2

God Would Rather Have Your Honest Heart Than Your Impressive Image

Ananias and Sapphira wanted the appearance of complete generosity without the honesty that genuine generosity required.

Their story reminds us that God is not impressed by the image we create for other people. God sees beyond appearances and desires truth in the deepest places of our lives.

Reflect Is there an area of my life where I am more concerned with how others see me than with being completely honest before God?

My Response

What is one step God may be inviting me to take this week toward greater generosity, honesty, or openness?
