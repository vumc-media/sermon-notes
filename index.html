The Power of a Planted Life
Psalm 92:12–15 | John 15:4–5
Big Idea
You grow where you're planted
  • If you want fruit, you need roots.
  • God’s not growing trees that keep uprooting.
Point 1
🌱 Planted People Flourish
Psalm 92:12–13
  • The righteous will flourish like a palm tree.
  • They grow strong like a cedar of Lebanon.
  • Those planted in the house of the Lord will flourish.
Am I planted or just passing through?
Point 2
🔗 You Cannot Bear Fruit Unless You Stay Connected to Jesus
John 15:4–5
  • Remain in me, and I will remain in you.
  • A branch cannot produce fruit by itself.
  • You must remain in the vine.
  • Without me, you can do nothing.
Am I truly connected to Jesus, or just nearby?
