Acts: The Church That Changed the World

Turning the World Upside Down

Sunday, November 8, 2026

Acts 17:16–34 (CEB)

Today's Scripture

Main Passage: Acts 17:16–34

Opening Context: Acts 17:1–9

Worship Reading: Acts 17:22–31

Scripture translation: Common English Bible (CEB)

In Athens, Paul encountered people with different beliefs and traditions. Rather than ignoring their culture, he used what he observed as an opportunity to share the good news of Jesus Christ.

Today's Big Idea

The early Church changed the world not by changing the Gospel, but by bringing its unchanging message into a changing world.

Point 1

Start Where People Are, Not Where You Wish They Were.

Acts 17:16–23 (CEB)

Paul took time to observe the people of Athens. He recognized their spiritual searching and used something familiar to them as a starting point for sharing the Gospel.

Key Takeaways

Personal Reflection

Am I meeting people where they are, or expecting them to become like me before I welcome them?

Point 2

Change Your Approach, Not Your Message.

Acts 17:24–31 (CEB)

Paul adapted the way he communicated with the Athenians, but he remained faithful to the truth of God as Creator and to the resurrection of Jesus Christ.

Key Takeaways

Personal Reflection

Am I holding tightly to the message of Jesus, or am I holding more tightly to the methods I have always used?

Putting Faith Into Action

The early Church was willing to go beyond familiar places, engage unfamiliar people, and boldly share the hope of Jesus.

This Week's Challenge

My Sermon Notes

Closing Thought

We don't have to change the Gospel to reach a changing world. We must be willing to change how we reach the world with the Gospel.
