First Called Christians

Acts 11:19–26

Welcome

In Antioch, the followers of Jesus were first called Christians. Their identity was connected to the One they followed.

Today we consider what it means to carry the name of Christ and allow His love to transform the way we live.

Acts 11:26 (KJV)
"...And the disciples were called Christians first in Antioch."

1. Following Jesus Should Change How We Live

Acts 11:19–21, 26

Following Jesus is more than identifying ourselves as Christians. Our relationship with Him should influence our choices, attitudes, priorities, and actions.

Supporting Scripture

2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)
"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."
James 1:22 (KJV)
"But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves."

Personal Reflection

1. If someone observed my daily life, what would they learn about Jesus?
2. What is one area of my life where Jesus is calling me to change?

2. When Jesus Changes Our Hearts, Love Becomes Our Response

Acts 11:22–26

A heart transformed by Christ should increasingly reflect His compassion, grace, patience, and love toward others.

Supporting Scripture

John 13:34–35 (KJV)
"A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another. By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another."
1 John 4:11 (KJV)
"Beloved, if God so loved us, we ought also to love one another."

Follow Along

Personal Reflection

3. Is there someone I need to approach with greater compassion or grace?
4. What is one practical way I can demonstrate the love of Jesus this week?
