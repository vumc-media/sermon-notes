You Are Still Loved

Romans 8:35-39 • Luke 15:11-24

Key Truth

God's love is not based on your performance, your past, your failures, or your ability to get everything right. His love pursues you, welcomes you home, and holds onto you even when life feels broken.

1. God’s Love Pursues You Before You Get Your Life Together

In the story of the Prodigal Son, the father did not wait for his son to become worthy before showing love. While the son was still far away, the father saw him, ran to him, embraced him, and welcomed him home. God's love moves toward us before we have everything figured out.

Reflection Question:
Have you ever felt like you needed to fix yourself before coming back to God?
Reflection Question:
What does the father's response in Luke 15 teach you about God's heart toward you?
Application:
God is not waiting for perfection. He is inviting you into relationship today.

My Notes

2. Nothing Can Separate You From God’s Love

Paul declares in Romans 8 that neither trouble, hardship, failure, suffering, death, life, powers, circumstances, nor anything else in all creation can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus.

Reflection Question:
What circumstances have tempted you to believe God has abandoned you?
Reflection Question:
Which phrase from Romans 8:35-39 speaks most deeply to your current season?
Application:
God's love is stronger than your worst day and greater than your greatest failure.
