ACTS: The Church That Changed the World

BOLD

What happens when ordinary people become completely confident in the presence and power of Jesus?

Acts 4:1–31
September 6, 2026

Today’s Message

Peter and John were ordinary men standing before powerful people. They were threatened, questioned, and commanded to stop speaking about Jesus.

But instead of retreating, the early church prayed for greater boldness.

Biblical boldness isn't pretending that we're not afraid. It is knowing who is with us and trusting God enough to obey Him even when obedience costs us something.

Point 1

Boldness Comes From Knowing Who Is With You

Acts 4:13–14, 31
Remember: Christian boldness isn't confidence in ourselves. It is confidence in Christ.

The religious leaders were amazed by Peter and John. They weren't impressed by their education, position, or influence. They recognized something much more important: these men had been with Jesus.

Courage grows when our attention shifts away from the size of the problem and toward the presence of the One who stands with us.

Reflect

Where am I allowing fear, uncertainty, or the opinions of others to keep me from faithfully following Jesus?

Point 2

Bold People Don't Pray for an Easier Life; They Pray for Greater Courage

Acts 4:23–31
Notice their prayer: the believers didn't ask God to remove every threat. They asked God to make them faithful in the middle of it.

After Peter and John were released, the church gathered to pray. They had every reason to ask God to make life easier.

Instead they prayed: “Enable your servants to speak your word with great boldness.”

Sometimes God's answer isn't to remove the difficult place. Sometimes His answer is to give us the courage to faithfully follow Him through it.

Reflect

What situation have I been asking God to remove when perhaps I should also be asking Him for courage to face it faithfully?

Take It With You

The question isn't whether we will ever feel afraid. The question is whether fear will have the final word.

The same God who empowered the first disciples is still present with His Church today.

My Step This Week

Where is God inviting me to choose faithful courage instead of comfortable silence?

Prayer:
Lord Jesus, remind me that I never stand alone. When fear makes me want to retreat, give me courage. When following You becomes uncomfortable, make me faithful. Give me the boldness to love, serve, speak, and live in a way that points others toward You. Amen.
