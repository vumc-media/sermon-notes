1. Envy Pollutes the Heart

James 3:14–16

“If you have bitter envy and selfish ambition in your heart… there you find disorder and every kind of evil.”

Envy is not harmless. Scripture exposes it as spiritual rot—earthly, unspiritual, and destructive.

Reflection:
Where might envy be hiding in my heart?

2. Envy Poisons Your Peace

Proverbs 14:30

“A peaceful heart gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones.”

Envy steals joy, contentment, and rest.

Reflection:
How has comparison affected my peace?

3. Envy Distorts Your Vision

Mark 15:9–11

Pilate knew Jesus was handed over out of envy.

Envy blinds us to God’s presence.

Reflection:
Is envy shaping how I see Jesus?

Closing Application

  • Rejoice with those who rejoice
  • Practice gratitude
  • Give thanks daily
