LOVE LIKE JESUS

Why Is There a Gap Between “Sunday Morning Me” and “Monday Morning Me”?

James 3:9-12 Galatians 5:16-25 John 13:34-35

Big Idea

Following Jesus is not measured only by how we worship on Sunday, but by how we reflect Him on Monday.

“By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.”
John 13:35
Opening Question:

If someone only observed my life Monday through Saturday, would they know I belong to Jesus?

1

Your Mouth Reveals Your Master

James 3:9-12

“With the tongue we praise our Lord and Father, and with it we curse human beings, who have been made in God’s likeness.”
James 3:9

James teaches us that our words are not just sounds coming from our mouths. They are signs revealing what is happening in our hearts.

A spring does not produce both fresh water and salt water. In the same way, a follower of Jesus should not praise God on Sunday and poison people with words on Monday.

Your mouth does not create your character. It reveals it.

Ask Yourself

Heart Check

Before asking, “How can I control my tongue?” ask:

“What is controlling my heart?”
My Notes:
2

Walk By The Spirit Before You Speak To People

Galatians 5:16-25

“So I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh.”
Galatians 5:16

Paul does not simply tell us to try harder. He tells us to walk by the Spirit.

The flesh reacts. The Spirit responds.

The Flesh Wants To...

  • Win the argument
  • Be right at all costs
  • Get even
  • Protect pride
  • Speak before praying

The Spirit Produces...

The fruit of the Spirit is not just Sunday fruit. It is Monday fruit.
Which fruit of the Spirit do I need most right now?
3

Love Isn’t A Feeling—It’s Your New Identity

John 13:34-35

“A new command I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another.”
John 13:34

Jesus does not describe love as a mood, preference, or emotion. He gives love as a command and a mark of identity.

People will not know we belong to Jesus simply because we attend church, sing worship songs, or know Bible verses.

Jesus said people would know we are His disciples by the way we love.

Love Like Jesus Means...

  • Loving people who disagree with you
  • Extending grace instead of revenge
  • Choosing forgiveness over resentment
  • Speaking truth without cruelty
  • Reflecting Christ when no one is watching

This week, I will ask the Holy Spirit to help me close the gap between Sunday morning and Monday morning.
