God Does His Deepest Work While We Wait

Waiting can feel unproductive, but God is often working beneath the surface in ways we cannot yet recognize. The waiting season may be part of the preparation.

Message Notes

Reflect

  1. Where in my life am I struggling to wait on God right now?
  2. What might God be developing in me during this season of waiting?
  3. Am I treating this season as wasted time, or as a place where God can still work?

God Is Preparing the Right People for the Right Moment

God does not only prepare circumstances. He also prepares people, relationships, gifts, and opportunities so that they come together at the right time.

Message Notes

Reflect

  1. How might God be preparing me for something I cannot fully see yet?
  2. Who has God placed around me during this season, and what might I learn from them?
