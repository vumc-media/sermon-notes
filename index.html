Acts: The Church That Changed the World

No More Outsiders

Acts 10:34–48

Sunday, October 18, 2026

Welcome to Today's Message

Throughout the book of Acts, we discover a Church being transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit.

In Acts 10, Peter discovers that God's grace is not confined by the boundaries people have established.

Today's Big Idea: The Gospel is not an exclusive invitation for a select few. Through Jesus Christ, God's grace reaches beyond the barriers that separate us.

Today's Scripture

Acts 10:34–48

Peter declares that God does not show favoritism. He proclaims the good news of Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit falls upon Gentile believers.

Read Acts 10:34–48 in the Bible App →

1 God's Grace Is Bigger Than Our Boundaries

Acts 10:34–35  |  Acts 10:44–45

Peter had grown up with deeply established religious and cultural boundaries. But God was teaching him to see people differently.

  • God does not show favoritism.
  • Our traditions and assumptions should never become barriers to sharing the love of Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit's work among the Gentiles demonstrated that God's grace extended beyond Peter's expectations.
Remember: God's grace is greater than the lines we sometimes draw between ourselves and others.

2 The Gospel Doesn't Wait for People to Come to Us

Acts 10:36–43  |  Acts 10:47–48

Peter did not remain within his familiar surroundings and wait for Cornelius to come to him. He entered the home of someone he once would have considered an outsider.

  • The Gospel is meant to be carried beyond the walls of the Church.
  • Sharing the love of Jesus requires us to meet people where they are.
  • The Church is called to be a living witness to Christ, not simply a place people are invited to attend.
Remember: The mission of the Church is not simply to invite people into a building, but to carry the hope of Christ into the world.

Living the Message

The story of Peter and Cornelius reminds us that God's Kingdom is larger than our comfort zones.

We are called not only to receive grace, but also to extend the love of Christ to others.

This Week's Challenge: Reach out to someone outside your normal circle. Offer encouragement, extend an invitation, or simply take time to listen and demonstrate the love of Jesus.
