This Is Just the Beginning

We’re Just Getting Started

These interactive sermon notes from Versailles United Methodist Church help you follow along with the message “This Is Just the Beginning: We’re Just Getting Started.” This sermon reflects on Philippians 1:6 and Acts 2:42–47, reminding us that God finishes what He starts and that the church grows when people show up fully.

Scripture: Philippians 1:6; Acts 2:42–47

1. God Finishes What He Starts

Philippians 1:6

God does not begin something and abandon it halfway through. What He starts, He continues. What He plants, He grows. What He calls into being, He completes.

Reflection:

Where do I need to trust that God is still working?

2. The Church Grows When People Show Up Fully

Acts 2:42–47

The early church was devoted—to teaching, fellowship, prayer, and generosity. They didn’t just attend… they belonged.

A growing church is not built by spectators. It’s built by people who show up fully.

Reflection:

Am I showing up partially or fully?

Closing Thought

This is not the end of the story. God is still working. God is still forming us. We’re just getting started.
