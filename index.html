Toy Story: You’ve Got a Friend in Me

Ecclesiastes 4:9–12 • John 15:12–17 • Proverbs 17:17 • Galatians 6:2

Big Idea

God never intended us to carry life alone. Real friendship reflects the faithful, sacrificial love Jesus has shown us.

Opening Question

Who has helped carry you through a difficult season of life?

1

Real Friends Help Carry What You Cannot Carry Alone

Ecclesiastes 4:9–12

“Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor: If either of them falls down, one can help the other up.”

Galatians 6:2

“Carry each other’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.”

Heart Check

Personal Reflection

What burden would become lighter if you invited someone to walk beside you?

2

Jesus Didn’t Just Command Friendship—He Modeled It

John 15:12–17

“My command is this: Love each other as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one’s life for one’s friends.”

Proverbs 17:17

“A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for a time of adversity.”

Heart Check

Personal Reflection

How can you intentionally love someone this week the way Jesus has loved you?

My Next Step

Who is one person God may be calling you to encourage, support, or walk beside this week?

Prayer

Jesus, thank You for loving us faithfully and calling us Your friends. Teach us to carry one another’s burdens, remain faithful in difficult seasons, and love others the way You have loved us. Amen.
