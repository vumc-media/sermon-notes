The Courage
to Love Well

1 Corinthians 13 | Micah 6:8

Opening Reflection

Love is not weakness. Love requires courage. It takes courage to show grace, extend mercy, seek understanding, and walk humbly with God and others.

“And now faith, hope, and love abide, these three; and the greatest of these is love.” — 1 Corinthians 13:13
Sermon Point 1

Love Is More Important Than What We Do

“If I speak... but do not have love, I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.” — 1 Corinthians 13:1

Paul reminds us that spiritual gifts, knowledge, faith, sacrifice, and religious activity mean little if love is absent.

Reflection Questions

Sermon Point 2

Love Is a Way of Life

“Love is patient; love is kind... It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.” — 1 Corinthians 13:4-7

Biblical love is more than a feeling. It is a daily decision about how we treat people when life is easy and when life is difficult.

Reflection Questions

Sermon Point 3

Love Leads Us Toward Maturity

“When I became an adult, I put an end to childish ways.” — 1 Corinthians 13:11

Growing in Christ means moving beyond self-centeredness and becoming more like Jesus in the way we think, speak, and respond.

Reflection Questions

Sermon Point 4

The Greatest Virtue Is Love

“And now faith, hope, and love abide, these three; and the greatest of these is love.” — 1 Corinthians 13:13

Everything else fades. Love remains because love reflects the very nature of God.

Living It Out

“I hope that either all of us, or none of us, are judged by the actions of our weakest moments.” — Ted Lasso
“What might this person be going through?” — Mr. Rogers

Final Challenge: Love in Action

“What does the Lord require of you but to do justice, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God?” — Micah 6:8

Choose one way to practice courageous love this week.

Closing Prayer

Lord, give us the courage to love well. Help us to be patient, kind, merciful, and humble. Teach us to see others through Your eyes and to live out Your love in practical ways every day. May our faith be known not only by what we believe, but by how we love. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
