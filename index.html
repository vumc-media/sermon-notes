INTO THE WILDERNESS: Led Into the Wilderness

Matthew 4:1–11 (CEB)

“Then the Spirit led Jesus up into the wilderness…” He went in led by the Spirit. He came out empowered by the Spirit. — Luke 4:14

The Wilderness Exposes False Identities

“If you are the Son of God…”

The first temptation wasn’t about bread. It was about identity. The wilderness strips away performance so you can rediscover identity.

The Wilderness Clarifies True Worship

“Worship the Lord your God, and serve Him only.”

The wilderness reveals what we really worship — control, comfort, outcomes, approval.

You Cannot Love Like Jesus Until You’ve Been Formed Like Jesus

Formation often happens in wilderness seasons — not in comfort, not in applause, but in hunger and dependence.

How Do We Love Like Jesus?

  1. Trust God in Hard Places
  2. Choose Obedience Over Shortcuts
  3. Anchor Identity in the Father’s Voice
  4. Worship God Above Outcomes

Personal Response

Where do you feel “in the wilderness” right now?
What shortcut is tempting you?
What would trusting God here look like?
