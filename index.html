INTO THE WILDERNESS: Hosanna or Crucify?

Matthew 21:1–11; 27:15–26
1. The Crowd Wanted a King Who Would Fix Their Problems
(Matthew 21:1–11)
The crowd welcomed Jesus with praise, but many were hoping for a king who would solve their immediate struggles. They wanted rescue on their terms. Sometimes we also want Jesus to fix circumstances without first changing our hearts.
Reflection Moment: What expectations have you placed on Jesus? Have you ever wanted Him to move according to your plan instead of trusting His purpose? Where might God be inviting you to surrender your version of rescue and receive His deeper work in your life?
2. When Jesus Didn’t Meet Expectations, the Crowd Turned on Him
(Matthew 27:15–26)
The same voices that cried “Hosanna” were later part of the cry for crucifixion. When Jesus did not match their expectations, admiration turned into rejection. Following Jesus means trusting Him even when He does not move the way we thought He would.
Reflection Moment: Have there been moments when your faith felt shaken because God did not respond the way you expected? How do disappointment, confusion, or delay affect your trust in Jesus? What would it look like to stay faithful even when you do not fully understand His way?
