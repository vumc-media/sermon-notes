Still Loved

When It Hurts Too Much to Pray

Scripture

Romans 8:26–28 | Psalm 34:18

1. God Meets You in the Silence

Romans 8:26–28

Reflection

Even when you don’t have the words, God is still listening and the Spirit is still moving.

Questions

  • Where have I run out of words with God?
  • Am I trying to perform in prayer instead of just being present?
  • Do I believe God is still near in my silence?

2. God Stays Close in the Breaking

Psalm 34:18

Reflection

God does not step away from brokenness—He draws near to it.

Questions

  • Where do I feel broken right now?
  • What am I trying to hide from God?
  • Can I trust that God is close even in pain?

Final Reflection

When it hurts too much to pray… God is still near.
