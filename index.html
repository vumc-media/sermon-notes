ACTS: The Church That Changed the World

THE CHURCH IS BORN

Week 4

Acts 2:22–47

Sermon Notes

The church wasn't born around a building, a program, or a style. It was born around Jesus.

As we look at the first believers in Acts 2, pay attention not only to what they believed, but also to how their belief changed the way they lived.

Sermon Point 1

A HEALTHY CHURCH IS CENTERED ON JESUS

Acts 2:22–36 Peter's message to the crowd centers on the life, death, resurrection, and lordship of Jesus Christ.

From its very beginning, the church's message was not, "Look at us."

The message was: "Look at Jesus."

Programs, ministries, traditions, worship styles, personalities, and buildings may all have a place in the life of a congregation, but none of them can become the center.

Jesus is the center.

Reflect

What can easily become more important to me than keeping Jesus at the center?
Where is Jesus inviting me to refocus my attention on Him?
Sermon Point 2

HEALTHY DISCIPLES DON’T JUST BELIEVE TOGETHER; THEY LIVE TOGETHER

Acts 2:42–47 The first believers devoted themselves to teaching, fellowship, breaking bread, prayer, generosity, worship, and life together.

The early church wasn't simply a group of people who happened to believe the same things.

Their faith created a community.

They learned together. They prayed together. They ate together. They worshiped together. They cared for one another.

Following Jesus moved beyond something they believed and became a life they shared.

Reflect

Am I simply attending church, or am I actively sharing life with the church?
What is one way I can become more intentional about encouraging, serving, or walking alongside someone in my church family?

My Response

A healthy church begins with healthy disciples— people centered on Jesus who choose to share life with one another.

What is God asking me to do because of what I heard today?
Prayer:

Jesus, keep You at the center of my life and at the center of Your church. Teach me not only to believe with other disciples, but to live, serve, pray, worship, and grow alongside them. Use my life to help build the kind of community we see in Acts. Amen.
