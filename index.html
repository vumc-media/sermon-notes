Can These Bones Live?

Ezekiel 37:1–14 | Interactive Sermon Notes

Scripture Focus

Ezekiel 37:3 — “Mortal, can these bones live?”

Ezekiel 37:14 — “I will put my spirit within you, and you shall live…”

1. The Bones Are Everywhere...
Ezekiel is brought into a valley filled with dry bones. Before God brings life, He first makes Ezekiel look honestly at what is broken, scattered, and lifeless.
Reflection Questions:
  • Where do I see dryness or discouragement in my life?
  • What situation feels too far gone to be restored?
  • Am I willing to let God show me the valley before He shows me the miracle?
2. Something Starts to Happen.
As Ezekiel speaks what God commands, the bones begin to come together. Restoration often begins before the full picture is complete.
Reflection Questions:
  • Where might God already be moving, even if I do not see the full result yet?
  • What step of obedience is God asking me to take?
  • Do I trust God enough to speak hope into dry places?
3. Hope Rises Up!
God does not simply organize the bones; He raises hope. What looked dead begins to stand again because God’s word is stronger than despair.
Reflection Questions:
  • What hope has God been trying to awaken in me?
  • Where have I accepted defeat too quickly?
  • How can I hold on to hope while I wait for God’s breath?
4. Life Returns!
The final miracle is not just movement, but breath. God puts His Spirit within His people, and what was dead becomes alive again.
Reflection Questions:
  • Where do I need God’s Spirit to breathe new life into me?
  • What would it look like for life to return in my faith, family, church, or community?
  • Do I believe God can still speak and act today?

Final Reflection

God does not look at dry bones and see the end. He sees what can live again when His Spirit begins to move.
