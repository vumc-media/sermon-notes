When It All Falls Apart: When Friends Don’t Help

Scripture: Job 3–31 •

Opening Reflection

Not every voice in pain deserves authority.

1) Not Every Voice Deserves a Microphone

Sometimes the most spiritual thing you can do is turn down the wrong voices.

  • Which voices are shaping your interpretation of pain?
  • Do those voices bring peace or pressure?
  • Which voice might need to be muted?

2) God Is Not Transactional; He Is Relational

God is not a formula to solve — He is a presence to trust.

  • Where have you treated faith like a transaction?
  • What would it look like to sit with God instead of solving Him?

Closing Thought

Faith stays in the conversation — even when answers don’t come.
