Faith in the Fog

Trusting God When You Can't See the Road Ahead

Still Loved Sermon Series

Psalm 23 | John 11:1–44

Big Idea
Faith is trusting God when the road ahead is hidden. Even when Jesus seems delayed, He is still present, still loving, and still working.
Point 1: God's Delays Are Not God's Denials

John 11:1–44

Mary and Martha thought Jesus was late. But Jesus was preparing them to see something greater than healing — resurrection.
Reflection Questions:
  • Where have you mistaken delay for denial?
  • What are you waiting on God to do?
  • How might God be working beyond what you can see?
Point 2: The Shepherd Walks With You Through the Valley

Psalm 23

Psalm 23 does not promise that we avoid the valley. It promises that the Shepherd walks with us through it.
Reflection Questions:
  • What valley are you walking through right now?
  • Where do you need to recognize God’s presence?
  • What is one faithful step you can take this week?
My Next Step
When you cannot see the whole road, follow the Shepherd one step at a time.
Prayer

Lord, when life feels uncertain and the road ahead is hidden, help me trust You. Remind me that Your delays are not denials and that Your presence never leaves me. Lead me one step at a time. Amen.
