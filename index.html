This Is Just the Beginning

The Power of a Planted Life
Psalm 92:12–15; John 15:4–5
Big Idea
You Grow Where You Are Planted
Psalm 92:12–15; John 15:4–5
God is not looking for shallow, passing-through faith. He desires rooted disciples—people who are planted in His presence, connected to Jesus, and growing in a way that produces lasting fruit. Growth does not happen by drifting. Fruit does not come without roots.
Key Line
  • If you want fruit, you need roots.
  • You grow where you’re planted.
  • God’s not growing trees that keep uprooting.

Vision Tie-In: Growth happens through discipleship pathways—classes, mentoring, Bible study, worship, and steady connection to the life of the church.

Main Point 1
Planted People Flourish
Psalm 92:12–13
Psalm 92 compares the righteous to strong, flourishing trees. They are not barely hanging on. They are alive, rooted, growing, and useful. The image is clear: people who are planted in the Lord do not live shallow lives. They become steady, fruitful, and strong because their lives are anchored in God.
Key Reflections
  • Planted people are not just present; they are rooted.
  • Spiritual flourishing is connected to staying near the Lord.
  • God grows people who stay grounded in Him.
  • The house of the Lord is not a stopping point; it is a planting place.

Reflection: Am I planted in the life of God, or only nearby?

Reflection: What does rooted faith look like in my daily life?

Reflection: Where have I been tempted to live as a spectator instead of someone planted?

Main Point 2
Planted People Keep Bearing Fruit
Psalm 92:14–15
The Psalm says planted people still bear fruit even when they are old and gray. That means a rooted life does not fade into uselessness. God’s people remain fresh, useful, and full of testimony. A planted life keeps proclaiming the faithfulness of God.
Key Reflections
  • Fruitfulness is not limited to one season of life.
  • God can keep using planted people for encouragement, witness, and ministry.
  • Freshness comes from being rooted in the Lord, not from age or appearance.
  • A fruitful life points back to the righteousness and goodness of God.

Reflection: What kind of fruit is God trying to grow in me right now?

Reflection: Is my life showing the faithfulness of God to others?

Reflection: Am I open to bearing fruit in every season, not just easy ones?

Main Point 3
You Cannot Bear Fruit Unless You Stay Connected to Jesus
John 15:4–5
Jesus says a branch cannot produce fruit by itself. It must remain in the vine. In the same way, our lives cannot produce real spiritual fruit apart from Him. Activity, busyness, and good intentions are not enough. Fruit comes from abiding—staying connected to Jesus in a real and ongoing way.
Key Reflections
  • Connection to Jesus is not optional; it is essential.
  • Fruit is the result of abiding, not pretending.
  • Without Jesus, we may look active but remain spiritually empty.
  • The closer we remain to Christ, the more fruit He produces through us.

Reflection: Am I remaining in Jesus, or just visiting Him occasionally?

Reflection: What habits help me stay connected to Christ?

Reflection: Where do I keep trying to produce fruit in my own strength?

Personal Response
Additional Reflection

Am I planted or just passing through?

Where is God calling me to put down deeper roots?

What does abiding in Jesus need to look like in this season of my life?

What fruit does God want to grow through me?

Weekly Challenge

This week, ask the Lord to deepen your roots. Commit to one practical step that keeps you planted— worship consistently, join a study, reconnect in prayer, begin mentoring, or spend intentional time in scripture. Ask yourself honestly: Am I planted, or just passing through?

Closing Prayer

Lord, plant me deeply in You. Keep me from living a shallow life that drifts from place to place without lasting fruit. Root me in Your presence, connect me more fully to Jesus, and grow in me what only You can grow. Make my life steady, fruitful, and faithful. Let my life proclaim that You are righteous, good, and trustworthy. Amen.
