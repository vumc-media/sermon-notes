INTO THE WILDERNESS: Led Into the Wilderness

Scripture: Matthew 4:1–11 (CEB) • Theme: Formation, identity, worship, obedience

Scripture Focus
Matthew 4:1–11 (CEB) “Then the Spirit led Jesus…”
The Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness where he was tempted. Jesus refuses to prove Himself, refuses to test God, and refuses to worship outcomes. He stands firm on God’s Word and comes out formed and strengthened.

Message Notes

Big idea (one sentence):
Point 1 — The Wilderness Exposes False Identities
Point 2 — The Wilderness Clarifies True Worship
Luke’s addition (Luke 4:14): Led in… Empowered out
How Do We Love Like Jesus? (practical steps)

Reflection & Response

Formation often happens in wilderness seasons. Use these prompts to respond honestly.

1) Where do you feel “in the wilderness” right now?
2) What “false identity” tends to show up under pressure?
3) What is your biggest shortcut temptation?
4) What would it look like to worship God above outcomes this week?
